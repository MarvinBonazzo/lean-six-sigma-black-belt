# Lean Six Sigma Black Belt: processes and people

An interactive summary of what I learned in the **Lean Six Sigma Black Belt** course (training manual by The Council for Six Sigma Certification), funded by the Learning Budget of [Bob W.](https://bobw.co/)

The idea: every tool is applied twice, once to hospitality processes and once to the way we work with people.

- **Live page:** https://marvinbonazzo.github.io/lean-six-sigma-black-belt/
- **Italian version:** https://marvinbonazzo.github.io/lean-six-sigma-black-belt/#it
- **PDF:** [Lean-Six-Sigma-Black-Belt.pdf](Lean-Six-Sigma-Black-Belt.pdf)

## The comic

The same lessons told as a short comic, step by step: [lean-six-sigma-black-belt/fumetto.html](https://marvinbonazzo.github.io/lean-six-sigma-black-belt/fumetto.html) (Italian and English; use the ← → arrow keys, or swipe on a phone). The story, characters and drawings are made up.

## What's inside

Seven modules plus a closing one, *What I bring to the team*:

1. What Lean Six Sigma is
2. Waste
3. Listen to whoever matters
4. Processes and teams
5. DMAIC
6. Statistics without fear
7. Decide together

Each module goes into the technical side in plain words: DPMO and sigma levels, rolled throughput yield, value stream maps, kanban, poka-yoke, Kano, CTQ trees, fishbone diagrams, takt time, Little's law, FMEA with RPN, control plans, DMADV, process capability (Cp and Cpk), control chart rules, measurement system analysis, hypothesis tests, regression, designed experiments, decision matrices and team stages.

Along the way: diagrams, hospitality examples and interactive parts:

- a hospitality / people view of the wastes
- a clickable 5 Whys
- a takt time calculator for housekeeping
- a process capability simulator: move the average and the spread and see Cp, Cpk and guests out of range

The PDF is the shorter, earlier version of the page.

Available in English and Italian.

## How it's made

- A single HTML file, no dependencies
- The Figtree font, loaded from Google Fonts
- No data is stored, except the chosen language in your browser
